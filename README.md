## 2. Key Analytical Observations
1. **Category Dominance:** Electronics drives maximum gross revenue due to high average ticket size, while Fashion leads in transactional frequency.
2. **Order Volume Distribution:** Delhi and Bangalore drive the largest transaction clusters, indicating higher regional penetration.
3. **Payment Preferences:** UPI and Credit Cards account for over 58% of gross transactional turnover, showing strong digital adoption.
4. **Repeat Customers:** Repeat purchasers drive significant aggregate revenue, highlighting strong customer retention opportunities.

---

## 3. Business Recommendations
- **Targeted Loyalty Programs:** Create targeted loyalty perks for repeat buyers in high-frequency categories.
- **Logistics Focus:** Monitor regional fulfillment partners in cities where non-delivery rates are higher.
